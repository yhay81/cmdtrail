# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
independent of identity, experience, background, location, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and evidence-oriented.
- Separate criticism of an observation claim from criticism of a person.
- Respect privacy; never post another person's secrets or operational metadata.
- Welcome corrections, especially when they reduce an overstated capability.
- Give credit for tests, reports, documentation, and review as well as code.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets,
sustained disruption, and retaliation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact available on
the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical and conflicts of interest will be
disclosed.
